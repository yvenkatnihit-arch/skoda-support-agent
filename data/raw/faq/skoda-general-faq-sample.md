# Škoda India general FAQ

<!-- SAMPLE document compiled on 2026-10-17 from skoda-auto.co.in and press reports. Replace with official FAQ pages. -->

## Which Škoda models are on sale in India?

Kylaq (compact SUV), Kushaq (mid-size SUV), Slavia (mid-size sedan), Kodiaq (full-size SUV with 5 or 7 seats) and Octavia RS (performance sedan). All of them are petrol cars. The Superb is still shown on the Škoda India website but is reported as discontinued, so check with a dealer.

## Does Škoda sell an electric car in India?

Not yet. The Enyaq electric SUV is expected in India, but Škoda has not announced its launch date, price or India specifications. Any figures you see are media estimates.

## How do I book a test drive?

Book a test drive online on the model page at skoda-auto.co.in (for example skoda-auto.co.in/shopping/kylaq-book-a-test-drive), at any Škoda showroom, or ask this assistant to arrange one for you.

## How do I get a brochure?

Download brochures from skoda-auto.co.in/shopping/download-a-brochure, or ask this assistant to send a brochure request.

## Where can I find a Škoda dealer?

Use the official dealer locator at dealers.skoda-auto.co.in, or tell this assistant your city, area or pincode to get the nearest showrooms and service centres.

## Are prices the same everywhere?

Ex-showroom prices can vary slightly between states, and the on-road price depends on your state's road tax, registration, insurance and other charges. Ask this assistant for an estimated on-road price for your city, and confirm the final price with the dealer.
