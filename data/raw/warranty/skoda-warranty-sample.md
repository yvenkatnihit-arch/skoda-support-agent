# Škoda Super Care: warranty and ownership package

<!-- SAMPLE document compiled on 2026-10-17 from skoda-auto.co.in/skoda-super-care/skoda-supercare and the model pages. Replace with the official warranty booklet or pages. -->

## Standard warranty

Škoda Super Care lists a 4-year / 100,000 km standard warranty. The Kushaq, Slavia and Kodiaq model pages state the same. Check the warranty booklet or your dealer for the exact terms for your car.

## Extended warranty

Extended warranty (EW) can extend cover up to the 6th year of ownership. Terms and prices are on skoda-auto.co.in/skoda-super-care/skoda-extended-warranty. Škoda also offers an Anytime Warranty, described at skoda-auto.co.in/skoda-super-care/anytime-warranty. Ask your dealer for the exact cover and cost for your car.

## Parts warranty

Škoda Super Care lists a parts warranty of 2 years or unlimited kilometres.

## Labour-free services

Škoda Super Care includes 4 labour-free services in the first two years. You still pay for parts and consumables such as engine oil and filters.

## Roadside assistance

New cars include 4 years of 24x7 Pan-India roadside assistance (Škoda Assist). Details: skoda-auto.co.in/service-and-parts/roadside-assistance.

## Maintenance plans

Škoda offers Maintenance Plans. The Super Care page does not list their contents or prices, so ask your dealer which plans are available for your model.
