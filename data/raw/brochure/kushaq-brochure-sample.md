## Overview

The Kushaq is Škoda's mid-size SUV, updated with a 2026 facelift. It has a 5-star Global NCAP safety rating. Trims: Classic+, Signature, Sportline, Prestige and Monte Carlo.

## Engines and gearboxes

The 1.0 TSI turbo-petrol (85 kW, 115 PS, 178 Nm) comes with a 6-speed manual or an 8-speed torque-converter automatic. The 1.5 TSI turbo-petrol (110 kW, 150 PS, 250 Nm) comes with a 7-speed DSG automatic and has disc brakes on all four wheels.

## Safety

Škoda lists 25 active and passive safety features as standard across the range, and up to 40 on the top variants. Every Kushaq has 6 airbags, ESC, ABS with EBD, traction control, rear parking sensors and ISOFIX mounts. Hill-hold assist is standard on the automatic Classic+ and on every Signature and above.

Signature adds a tyre pressure monitoring system, a rear parking camera, front and rear parking sensors and LED fog lamps with a cornering function. Prestige adds an anti-theft alarm.

## Comfort and convenience

Classic+ already has automatic climate control with an air purifier and rear vents, an electric sunroof and rain-sensing wipers. Signature adds keyless entry, cruise control, a cooled glove box and 60:40 split rear seats. Prestige adds a panoramic sunroof, powered and ventilated front seats, a rear seat massage function and beige leatherette upholstery. Monte Carlo has a black cabin with red trim and red stitching.

## Infotainment

Classic+ has a 7-inch touchscreen with wired Android Auto and Apple CarPlay and 6 speakers. Signature adds a 10.1-inch touchscreen with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, an 8-inch digital driver's display and a 15W wireless phone charger. Prestige adds a Škoda sound system with subwoofer and amplifier.

## Exterior

Every Kushaq has automatic LED headlamps, LED tail lamps and 16-inch alloy wheels. Signature gets 17-inch silver alloys and an illuminated light band on the grille. Sportline has 17-inch black alloys and Sportline badging. Monte Carlo has 17-inch dual-tone alloys, red grille stripes and Monte Carlo badges.
