## Overview

The Kylaq is Škoda's compact SUV for India, under 4 metres long. It has a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating. It is sold in seven trims: Classic, Classic+, Signature, Signature+, Sportline, Prestige and Prestige+.

## Engine and gearbox

Every Kylaq uses the 1.0 TSI turbo-petrol engine with 85 kW (115 PS) and 178 Nm. It comes with a 6-speed manual or a 6-speed torque-converter automatic. The automatic is available from the Classic+ trim upwards; the base Classic is manual only.

## Safety

Standard on every trim: 6 airbags, ABS with EBD, Electronic Stability Control (ESC), traction control, brake disc wiping, roll-over protection, rear parking sensors and ISOFIX child-seat mounts.

Signature adds a tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS). Signature+ adds hill-hold assist and a rear parking camera. Sportline adds LED fog lamps with a cornering function. Prestige adds an anti-theft alarm.

## Comfort and convenience

Classic+ adds a single-pane electric sunroof, cruise control, an auto-dimming inside mirror, steering-mounted controls and one-touch driver window. Signature adds rear AC vents, a cooled glove box, rain-sensing wipers, a rear wiper and washer, and paddle shifters on the automatic. Signature+ adds keyless entry and go, a leather-wrapped steering wheel and 60:40 split-folding rear seats. Prestige adds ventilated front seats and leatherette upholstery. Prestige+ adds 6-way powered front seats.

## Infotainment

Signature has a 7-inch touchscreen with wired Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. Signature+ upgrades to a 10-inch touchscreen with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, an 8-inch digital instrument cluster and connected-car features. Sportline and Prestige add a wireless phone charger.

## Exterior

Classic has 16-inch steel wheels. Signature gets 16-inch silver alloy wheels. Sportline has 17-inch black alloys, a rear spoiler and LED projector headlamps. Prestige has 17-inch dual-tone alloys and LED projector headlamps.
