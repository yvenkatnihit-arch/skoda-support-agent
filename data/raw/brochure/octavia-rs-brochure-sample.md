## Overview

The Octavia RS is Škoda's performance sedan, imported in limited numbers. It comes in one fully loaded version. Colours: Mamba Green, Magic Black and Velvet Red.

## Engine and performance

A 2.0 TSI turbo-petrol engine with 195 kW (265 PS) and 370 Nm drives the front wheels through a 7-speed DSG automatic. It reaches 0-100 km/h in 6.4 seconds, and top speed is electronically limited to 250 km/h.

## Safety and driver assistance

The Octavia RS has 10 airbags, adaptive cruise control, Front Assist, Lane Assist, blind spot detection, ESC, a 360-degree Area View camera and Intelligent Park Assist.

## Comfort and infotainment

Heated, power-adjustable front sports seats with memory and massage, a head-up display, a Virtual Cockpit, three-zone Climatronic air conditioning and ambient lighting. A 32.77 cm touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a Canton 675W sound system, wireless charging and USB-C ports.

## Exterior

Full LED Matrix headlights with Adaptive Front Lighting, LED rear lights with animated indicators, RS bumpers, a sports exhaust with two visible tailpipes and gloss black accents.
