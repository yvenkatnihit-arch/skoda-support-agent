## Overview

The Slavia is Škoda's mid-size sedan, updated for 2026. It has a 5-star Global NCAP safety rating. Trims: Classic, Classic+, Signature, Sportline, Prestige and Monte Carlo. The 1.5 TSI engine is offered only on Prestige and Monte Carlo.

## Engines and gearboxes

The 1.0 TSI turbo-petrol (85 kW, 115 PS, 178 Nm) comes with a 6-speed manual or an 8-speed torque-converter automatic. The 1.5 TSI turbo-petrol (110 kW, 150 PS, 250 Nm) comes with a 7-speed DSG automatic and Active Cylinder Technology.

## Safety

Standard on every Slavia: 6 airbags, stability control, traction control, a tyre pressure monitor, ISOFIX mounts and rear parking sensors. Classic+ automatic adds hill-hold control. Signature adds a rear-view camera and front and rear parking sensors. Sportline adds a 360-degree camera.

## Comfort and convenience

Classic has automatic climate control, a height-adjustable driver's seat, power windows and auto start/stop. Classic+ adds a single-pane sunroof, cruise control, rain-sensing wipers, an auto-dimming mirror and paddle shifters on the automatic. Signature adds ambient lighting, keyless entry, push-button start, 60:40 split folding rear seats, a cooled glove box and rear AC vents. Prestige adds powered and ventilated front seats, a rear seat massage function and leatherette upholstery.

## Infotainment

Classic has a 7-inch touchscreen with wired smartphone connectivity and 4 speakers. Signature adds a 10.1-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a Google AI assistant, an 8-speaker sound system and a wireless phone charger. Prestige adds a 10.25-inch digital driver's display.

## Exterior

Classic has halogen headlamps and 15-inch steel wheels. Classic+ adds 16-inch alloy wheels, full-LED headlamps, LED fog lamps and sequential rear indicators. Sportline has 16-inch black alloys and a boot lip spoiler. Monte Carlo adds red grille accents and red brake calipers.
