## Overview

The second-generation Kodiaq is Škoda's full-size SUV. Trims: Lounge, Sportline and Selection L&K. Lounge is a 5-seater; Sportline and Selection L&K are 7-seaters.

## Engine and gearbox

Every Kodiaq uses a 2.0 TSI turbo-petrol engine with 150 kW (204 PS) and 320 Nm, a 7-speed DSG automatic and 4x4.

## Safety

The Kodiaq has up to 9 airbags (the count depends on the variant), ESC, ABS, a multi-collision brake, hill descent control and driver attention alert. Sportline and Selection L&K add ADAS features (Front Assist, Side Assist and Exit Warning), a 360-degree camera and Intelligent Park Assist.

## Comfort and infotainment

The Kodiaq has a 32.77 cm (12.9-inch) touchscreen and a 26.03 cm (10.25-inch) Virtual Cockpit. Selection L&K adds ventilated and massaging front seats. Matrix LED headlights are fitted.
