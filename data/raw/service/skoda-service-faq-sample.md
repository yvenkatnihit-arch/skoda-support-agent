## How often should my car be serviced?

New Škoda cars get two free inspections, at 1,000 km or 1 month and at 7,500 km or 6 months. After that, periodic maintenance is due every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

## What is done at a periodic service?

Every periodic service replaces the engine oil, oil filter, drain plug washer and cabin filter, and includes sunroof lubrication on cars with a sunroof. Every 30,000 km the air filter and brake fluid are also replaced. At 60,000 km the spark plugs and V-belt are replaced. Automatic (torque-converter) cars also get an automatic transmission fluid (ATF) change at 60,000 km, which makes that service noticeably more expensive.

## How do I book a service?

Book online at skoda-auto.co.in/service-and-parts/book-service-appointment, or call your nearest Škoda service centre. You can find service centres, their phone numbers and opening hours with the dealer locator at dealers.skoda-auto.co.in, or ask this assistant for the nearest one.

## What services do service centres offer?

Authorised Škoda service centres handle periodic maintenance, repairs and genuine accessories. Some outlets are combined showroom and service centres.

## What should I do if my car breaks down?

Call Škoda Assist, the 24x7 Pan-India roadside assistance included for 4 years with new cars. Details are at skoda-auto.co.in/service-and-parts/roadside-assistance.
